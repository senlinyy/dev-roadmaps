title: "Inspect the Legacy Orders VM"
sectionSlug: identity-network-and-repeatability
order: 2
---

Inspect `orders-legacy-01` in zone `europe-west2-b`. Confirm its state, machine type, VPC and subnet, runtime service account, and whether it has an external IP.

The application is intended to be private. Use the resource evidence to determine whether its network attachment matches that boundary.
