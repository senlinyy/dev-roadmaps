---
title: "Inspect the Production Network"
sectionSlug: global-network-regional-subnets
order: 2
---

Inspect the global `orders-prod` VPC and its regional `orders-apps-europe-west2` subnet. Verify the routing mode, whether automatic subnet creation is disabled, the subnet CIDR, region, and Private Google Access setting.

The evidence should let a reviewer distinguish global network properties from regional subnet properties.
