The completed website project is in `/workspace`. Run the local safety sequence and finish with a saved plan showing three creates and no destructive action.

Your job:

1. **Format** the configuration.
2. **Initialize** providers and working data.
3. **Validate** the configuration.
4. **Save** a plan as `website.tfplan` and confirm three resources will be added.

The grader checks the command workflow and resulting Terraform state, not a prose explanation.
